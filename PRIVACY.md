# Privacy policy

**Last updated:** November 8, 2026

This policy covers the websites built from this repository: rgdevment.com,
www.tisty.app, www.linkunbound.app and www.copanda.app. Each app has its own
privacy policy in its repository.

## The short version

The sites have no accounts, no forms, no cookies and no advertising. They are
static pages; nothing you do on them is stored by us.

## Counting visits

- **www.tisty.app** counts no visits: it loads no analytics of any kind.
- **The other sites** count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, without
  cookies or identifiers.

## What stays in your browser

- **Light or dark.** When you pick one with the button at the top, the site
  keeps your choice in your browser's local storage, so the next page opens the
  same way. It is not a cookie and never leaves your browser; clearing the
  site's data forgets it.
- **Searching the docs.** The search box reads an index downloaded from the
  same site and runs in your browser. What you type is not sent anywhere.

## Hosting

The sites are served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare
handles your IP address to deliver the page and protect the service, under its
own privacy policy. We do not receive or keep those logs.

## Links out

Downloads, documentation sources and support links lead to GitHub and other
sites, which have their own policies.
